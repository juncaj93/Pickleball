## 5. Daylight saving (new permanent test)

`tests/dst-2027.test.ts`, 16 tests, all green. Fake clock around **Sun Mar 14, 2027** (spring forward) and **Sun Nov 7, 2027** (fall back), each with the days around them, through the real server:

- The real 5-minute clock ticked every 5 minutes for 9.5 days around each change with a stub Telegram: booking reminders at 6:45 and 7:30 AM (also for games on the other side of the change), last call 7:00 PM the night before, the hour-before text at 7:00 PM, the cancel warning at 10:00 PM and its follow-up at 10:30 PM, Finalize at 10:30 PM. Each sent exactly once, nothing in the night.
- Venue charge days start at 12:00 AM Eastern on the change day; a game counts by its Eastern date (an 8:00 PM game whose UTC date is the next day stays in its own period).

I proved the test can fail: with four deliberate errors put into the clock code (plain 24-hour math for the booking day and the last call, UTC dates for the day words) it went red, and green again with the real code.

**No real daylight saving bug found.** One thing to know (question for Alex, not counted): the cancel-by time is 12 real hours before the start. For a game that starts on a clock-change Sunday before about 2:00 PM, the wall clock moves by an hour: a 10:00 AM game on Sun Mar 14, 2027 says "Cancel by Sat 9:00 PM", and on Sun Nov 7, 2027 "Sat 11:00 PM". Tue and Thu games never see this.

## 6. Backups and restore (read-only drill)

The four export paths (Settings > Backup download, the weekly copy in one run, the weekly copy in steps, and a deploy export) hold the same data. A restore into a fresh database with the project's own restore script gives back every row of every backed-up table; money totals, the 21 health checks and foreign keys match. The guest columns (migrations 0021 and 0022) are in every export, and older backups without them restore into today's database.

### What is NOT in the backup file or weekly copy
- **task_log** (emptied on restore): every Settings > Feature switch goes OFF; Smart alerts comes back ON; **finalized games lose their price locks** (M-2); Venue months marked paid are forgotten and unpaid due cards vanish once the switch is back on; the Christmas choice and Alex's own theme choice are gone; canceled games hidden from players show again; "Do this now" taps and per-game marks are forgotten; "already sent" marks for smart alerts, the hour-before text and Second Chance are forgotten, so an alert due in the catch-up hour can go out again.
- **organizer_alerts** (emptied): every Telegram alert type comes back ON.
- **admin_sessions** (deleted): every organizer and read-only sign-in is signed out.
- **login_attempts, rate_events**: not in the file and not cleared; counters carry on.
- **d1_migrations**: not in the file; only matters for a new empty database (run the migrations first).
- Phones that picked a name after the backup must pick again.
- The read-only PIN download also blanks the PIN hash and group word (cannot be restored, F-11) and the History phone tags.

Time Travel and the deploy export leave out nothing (a deploy export loads only into an empty database).
