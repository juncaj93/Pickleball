## 5. Daylight saving (new permanent test)

`tests/dst-2027.test.ts`, 16 tests, all green. Fake clock around **Sun Mar 14, 2027** (spring forward) and **Sun Nov 7, 2027** (fall back), each with the days around them, through the real server:
- Every game stays 8:00 to 10:00 PM Eastern on a Tue or Thu at every moment walked (including 1:59 AM then 3:00 AM in spring and both 1:30 AMs in fall), none doubled; the player screen and the calendar file agree.
- Cancel-by is 8:00 AM on game day; the red card line reads "tomorrow" then "today" at the right minutes; a drop at 7:59 AM is not late and at 8:00 AM it is, with Late fees off and on ($14 owed at 3 left).
- "tonight", "tomorrow", the "Want in?" weekday and THIS WEEK / NEXT WEEK follow the Eastern calendar day, including Monday evening when UTC is already Tuesday.
- The real 5-minute clock ticked every 5 minutes for 9.5 days around each change with a stub Telegram: booking reminders at 6:45 and 7:30 AM (also for games on the other side of the change), last call 7:00 PM the night before, the hour-before text at 7:00 PM, the cancel warning at 10:00 PM and its follow-up at 10:30 PM, Finalize at 10:30 PM. Each sent exactly once, nothing in the night.
- Venue charge days start at 12:00 AM Eastern on the change day; a game counts by its Eastern date (an 8:00 PM game whose UTC date is the next day stays in its own period).

I proved the test can fail: with four deliberate errors put into the clock code (plain 24-hour math for the booking day and the last call, UTC dates for the day words) it went red, and green again with the real code.

**No real daylight saving bug found.** One thing to know (question for Alex, not counted): the cancel-by time is 12 real hours before the start. For a game that starts on a clock-change Sunday before about 2:00 PM, the wall clock moves by an hour: a 10:00 AM game on Sun Mar 14, 2027 says "Cancel by Sat 9:00 PM", and on Sun Nov 7, 2027 "Sat 11:00 PM". Tue and Thu games never see this.
