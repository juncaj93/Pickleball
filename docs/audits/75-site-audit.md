## MUST FIX (2)
1. **Money:** Answer "Paid" on an unconfirmed I paid from a player who then dropped late (game not left short) toasts "Rowan's $14 is credit now"
   but gives no credit; Clear > My choice then says "cleared" and still gives nothing. Cause: `recordLateDrop` (server/roster.ts:776) stamps
   `late_drop_at` with `played` left NULL, and `OPEN_FEE` (server/lateFees.ts:58) evaluates to NULL, not false, so `NOT OPEN_FEE` in
   server/credit.ts:252 skips the seat (same fragment at server/admin.ts:1437, server/roster.ts:801). The agreement test missed it because it
   compares `sql === 1` (tests/late-fees.test.ts:782). Fix: `COALESCE(played, -1) = 0` in OPEN_FEE (and OPEN_LATE_DROP), and a test with
   played NULL. Also check live for seats already stuck this way (SELECT only).
2. **Words / money truth:** the late Drop out dialog tells a player who already paid "If no one takes your spot, you still owe $14."
   (shared/lateFees.ts:165, shared/guestSpot.ts:168, src/pages/PlayerHome.tsx:3242/3251/3260 ignore `lateDrop.paid`). Fix: when paid, "Your $14
   stays on this game unless someone takes your spot." (the toast's words).
