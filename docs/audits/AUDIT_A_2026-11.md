## FIX SOON

### A-01: A game does not remember the price rules it was finalized under (money)
- What happens: if you turn Short games OFF (it is ON on live), every past 1.5 hour game is re-priced on Money > Totals as a 2 hour game.
  - Court cost goes from $36 to $48, so "your cost so far" goes up $12 per game.
  - A Reopen and Finalize while it is off prices the game at the 2 hour table. Players get less credit back than they should.
  - Booking hours behaves the same way for 2.5 and 3 hour games once it is used.
  - So does changing Settings > Money "Typical court cost": every past game with no cost typed in follows the new number. That last one is from reading the code, not a run.
- Where:
  - `server/repo.ts:148-150` (priceOf) and `:371-373` (courtCostOf), `shared/gameLength.ts:114`, `server/money.ts:143, 160, 167`.
  - `server/admin.ts:2333-2350`: the switch only re-prices games that are not finalized, and nothing protects finalized ones.
  - Screens: Money > Totals (So far, Projected, per court, Month by month), the Money game row, and the organizer's "Spent" on Players.
- How to see it (run on a local copy): Short games on, a 1.5 hour game with 6 players at $11 cash, finalized at $7 ($4 back each).
  - Totals: court $36, your cost $24.
  - Turn Short games off: court $48, your cost $36. No player money moved.
  - Then Reopen and Finalize: $9 each and $2 back each, instead of $7 and $4. That is $12 of credit the 6 players should have had.
  - A second reviewer saw the same with a 2.5 hour game ($60 court shows as $48; a redo gives $9 each instead of $12).
- Fix idea: save the court cost and the length row on the game when it is finalized (or created), and read those saved values afterwards.
- Until fixed: do not turn Short games off. Do not change "Typical court cost" if past months' Totals matter to you.
- Confidence: sure.

### A-02: A price raised after cash payments is never collected when the game ends in a late fee (money)
- What happens: you change a paid 1.5 hour game to 2 hours (or later a 2 hour game to 2.5 or 3 hours). The players who paid cash at the old price still show "paid, owes $0". This is the documented rule: the gap is settled at Finalize.
  - If that game is instead canceled inside 12 hours and you answer No to "Did the venue waive the fee?", nobody is ever asked for the gap.
  - The same is likely for a paid late drop and a paid no-show, which read the seat as already paid (from the code, not run).
- Where:
  - `server/admin.ts:1049-1050` (Edit game re-prices only seats paid with credit).
  - `server/views.ts:83-85, 127, 244`: what is owed is only worked out for unpaid seats.
  - `server/credit.ts:428`: the fee split does nothing when a seat holds less than the split price.
- How to see it (run on a local copy, live's switches): Late drops owe $14 on, Late-cancel fee split off. A 1.5 hour game with 5 players who paid $11 cash.
  - Edit game to 2 hours: each seat shows share $14, owes $0.
  - Cancel 3 hours before, venue kept the fee: still owes $0 each, and the player's Pay card is empty. That is $15 never asked for.
  - With the fee split on and 5 in, the split price is $11, so there is no gap. With 4 in it is $3 each.
  - A 2 hour game made 3 hours (Booking hours on) leaves $35 for 5 players, or $10 with the split.
- Until fixed: avoid changing the length of a game people already paid for. If you do, and it then cancels late, check the Money row by hand.
- Confidence: sure for the late cancel, likely for late drop and no-show.

## Cost: database reads

Measured on a local copy of the fake preview world (123 games):
- Each "anything new?" check (every open phone, every 5 seconds) runs 12 statements with Fewer database trips on, as on live.
- Three of them read every game the group ever had, and only then drop the old ones. The only index on games starts with the group, not the end time.
- So each check reads about 3 more rows for every game ever added. At about 2.3 games a week, that is about 110 games, or about 330 more rows per check, each year.

The proposal's own measurement on live (81 rows per check with 8 games, about 28% of the free 5 million daily rows on a busy game day) puts the free limit at about 290 rows per check. That is about 70 more games, roughly 7 months away at today's pace.

The index (`CREATE INDEX ... ON sessions (group_id, ends_at)`) keeps those three reads at the games of the last 90 days, about 30, however long the season runs. It is worth doing in the next quiet window with a database change. It is not urgent today.

Other reads:
- Each full player page load also reads every credit record of the group (no index on group) and every Activity row of the group, twice (for the late-fee notices). These run only when the "anything new?" answer changes, so they are smaller.
- Organizer dashboard: 44 statements with Fewer database trips on (85 with it off).
