## 4. Toasts, alerts and sheets that name a day or time

Player and organizer toasts and cards use the card's own date and time (for example "Tue Nov 17, 8:00 PM") and the cancel-by time ("start minus 12 hours"). A read of the sources found no toast that says "today" or "tonight" for a game on another day. The blue TONIGHT strip and TONIGHT / TOMORROW date tags are tied to the Eastern calendar day.

## 5. Findings (nothing changed from these)

1. **Game day text always says "tonight"**, even for a game before 5 PM or a 12 AM to 2 AM game (sample: "Pickleball tonight @ 12:30am!"). The other texts say "today" before 5 PM. It is only sent an hour before a game, so it is never the wrong day, but a lunchtime game would still read "tonight".
2. **A 12 AM to 2 AM game reads "tomorrow" the evening before** (Mon 11 PM for a Tue 12:30 AM game). It follows the Eastern calendar day as designed, but some players may think of it as Monday night.
3. **"Join tonight" and "Do it tonight" in Need players and Check-in** (new and last-call wording) are about the deadline, so they read "tonight" for a game tomorrow and "early" for one further out. Correct by design, but "Join tonight" for a Tue game texted on a Sunday evening is the only odd case.
4. **Old wording inconsistencies** (only shows with the Texts round 2 switch off): "Pickleball Fri, Nov 13 is final" has a comma that the new wording does not, and the cancel text reads "...is canceled. Rain." with the reason as its own sentence.
5. **Moved (new) names the old day** ("Pickleball tonight is moved to tomorrow @ 8pm."). Correct, since it names the game that was moved, but it can read twice as a day word.
6. **Money page Text all** reads "Tue Nov 17's pickleball" for a game further out (and "today's"/"tonight's" nearer). Correct, a little clunky next to the weekday wording used in Text the players.
7. **Text the players now says the weekday ("Tuesday's game")** while Share and Need players say the date ("Tue Nov 17"). Alex asked for exactly this; both are clear, but they are two different styles.
8. **Late cancel texts (old wording) assumed game day.** A cancel inside 12 hours can be the night before for a morning game, which read "today" wrongly. Fixed with this zip; the new wording was already right.
9. **Drop alerts only fire inside 36 hours.** A Tuesday game dropped on Friday sends no Telegram. By design (Smart alerts).
10. **Extra time alerts** use "tonight's" for any game not yet past (their wording assumes they are shown after the game). Harmless today because they only appear after play.
11. **Time zone:** every sample used Eastern time with AM/PM (never UTC). No UTC or 24-hour times found.
12. **Punctuation:** no doubled punctuation, no em dashes, address alone on the last line after a blank line in every new-wording text. The old-wording texts put the address on the same line after a period, as they always did.

## What Zip 40 changed

Only the day word in four builders, using the Eastern calendar day: today keeps the old words exactly; tomorrow says "tomorrow's game"; any other day says the weekday ("Tuesday's game").
